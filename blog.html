Essential Travel Checklist

Before every international trip, taking a few minutes to prepare can save you hours of stress. Follow these simple tips to ensure your journey starts smoothly.

  • Double-check your passport validity.
  • Keep printed copies of important documents.
  • Arrive at the airport at least 3 hours before departure.
  • Inform your bank before international travel.
  • Purchase travel insurance.
  • Save emergency contacts.
