Our History

Founded by a passionate team with extensive experience in travel and education consulting, Paveway Travels and Tours officially launched in 2023, with operations based in Nigeria. Our mission is clear: to connect people with new opportunities, cultures, and experiences across borders.

Our journey began behind the scenes, collaborating with established travel and educational agencies. Over time, we naturally transitioned into assisting friends and family with flight bookings and securing admissions abroad. As demand grew—particularly among Africans seeking honest and reliable guidance for international travel and study—we recognized the need for a more transparent and dependable service.

This realization inspired us to make it official.

Today, Paveway Travels offers a wide range of services, including flight bookings, curated tour packages, and comprehensive study abroad support, especially for Schengen countries. What sets us apart is our commitment to integrity, transparency, and trust—no hidden fees, no confusion, just clear and reliable support tailored to your needs.
