FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your visa or study abroad journey with Paveway Travels & Tours.

How long does visa processing take?

Processing times vary depending on the country and visa category. Our team will always provide an estimated timeline based on your application.

Do you assist with university admissions?

Yes. We help students choose suitable institutions, process admissions, prepare documents and guide them through their visa applications.

Can I apply from anywhere in Nigeria?

Absolutely. We provide online consultations and support clients across Nigeria throughout the entire application process.

How do I get started?

Simply contact our team through WhatsApp, phone or our contact page. We'll discuss your goals and guide you on the next steps.
